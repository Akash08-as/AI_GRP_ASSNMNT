Ans.3:-Bayesian Estimation using the BDeu prior generally performs better than Maximum Likelihood Estimation for small datasets. MLE estimates probabilities directly from the observed frequencies, making it sensitive to limited or missing observations. In contrast, Bayesian Estimation uses prior information to smooth the probability estimates, reducing the effect of sparse data and preventing extreme probability values. Therefore, BDeu estimation is generally more reliable when the training dataset is small. As the dataset size increases, MLE and Bayesian estimates tend to become closer because more observations provide better estimates of the actual probabilities.
